Using it with Meta glasses: pair your glasses with your phone as usual, open this page, pick a spot or tour, then put your phone in your pocket. Turn-by-turn directions and the story behind each piece play through the glasses' speakers. Turn on Voice commands and say "next", "repeat", "how far" or "what is this". If your glasses have a display, turn on Glasses display view for a minimal black heads-up view.

Street art changes all the time, so a piece may have been painted over. Watch for traffic and stay aware of your surroundings. Routes © OpenStreetMap contributors (FOSSGIS routing).
