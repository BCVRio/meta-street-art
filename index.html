How it works: SprayPath uses your phone's camera, GPS and compass. Pink arrows flow along the pavement towards the next turn, and a blue light beacon marks where the artwork is. On supported Android phones, tap Floor lock to pin the arrows to the ground using ARCore.

Tips: hold the phone upright. If the arrows point the wrong way, move the phone in a figure of eight to calibrate the compass. GPS is accurate to about 5–15 m between tall buildings. Look up from the screen when crossing roads.

Street art changes all the time, so a piece may have been painted over. Routes © OpenStreetMap contributors (FOSSGIS routing). Built with A-Frame, free and open source.
